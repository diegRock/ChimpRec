Interpreting the Results for your Chimps
Since you are manually correcting the Ground Truth, you likely care about specific aspects of performance. Here is how to interpret the output:

DetA (Detection Accuracy): How good is YOLO?
If this is low, YOLO is missing chimps or drawing boxes in empty space.
DetRe (Recall): Are you finding all the chimps?
DetPr (Precision): Are you avoiding detecting rocks/trees as chimps?
AssA (Association Accuracy): How good is DeepSORT?
If this is low, your tracker is confusing chimps (ID Switches). E.g., It tracks "MUKE", loses him, then picks him up again as a "New ID", or swaps "MUKE" with another chimp.
HOTA: The geometric mean of DetA and AssA. This is your single summary score.
Note on Empty Frames: The parser logic assumes the # delimiter marks the end of a frame. If your file format has multiple # symbols in a row for empty frames, or different spacing, the parse_chimp_file function might need slight adjustment, but it is currently robust to standard formatting.
